# Faith Cluster SEO Map

The Faith cluster is designed to establish branded entity ownership first. It does not pretend that fictional proper nouns already have meaningful non-brand search demand.

| URL | Query ownership | Search role | Must not duplicate |
|---|---|---|---|

## Internal-linking rules

- Every faith article links to `/faith/` and `/faith/what-is-ateshism/` through breadcrumbs or body copy.
- The cornerstone links once to every major faith entity using descriptive anchors.
- Specialist pages link laterally only where the relationship is explained in the surrounding sentence.
- Do not insert repetitive exact-match links into every paragraph.
- Hub-card copy and article introductions must remain distinct; do not reuse the same summary verbatim across templates.

## Measurement

Primary early signals:

- correct indexing and canonical selection;
- branded impressions for Ateshism, Mehr Arahita, Eternal Flame and the sacred books;
- discovery-page-assisted entrances into the Faith cluster;
- hub-to-article click depth;
- article-to-book assisted visits;
- natural backlinks to the Five Pillars, founder, debate and visual-diagram pages.

Broad non-brand acquisition belongs mainly to `/blog/` and linkable assets. Lore pages build entity depth, reader trust and conversion journeys; they should not be distorted into generic listicles to chase volume.
