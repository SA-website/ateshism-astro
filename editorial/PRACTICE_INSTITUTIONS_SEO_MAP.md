- `/faith/body/` owns the theology of Sacred Embodiment; `/practice/unbound-chest/` owns its clothing application.
- `/faith/consent/` owns the doctrine; practice pages show how it limits touch, ritual, dress and institutional access.
- `/faith/fire/` owns fire symbolism; `/practice/prayer-urn/` owns one ritual use.
- `/institutions/temple-system/` owns the network; `/practice/temple-etiquette/` answers visitor questions.
- `/institutions/ash-order/` owns the organisation; `/institutions/ash-mothers/` owns the vocation; `/institutions/ash-ranks/` owns the hierarchy.
- `/institutions/rose-mothers/` owns the priesthood; the future `/institutions/male-ordination/` page will own the ordination controversy.

## On-page SEO rules

1. Retain the fiction notice near the beginning of every page.
2. Use the primary entity naturally in the title, first paragraph and one useful subheading; do not force repeated exact-match phrasing.
3. Keep FAQ content only where it answers a genuine ambiguity. Do not repeat the same FAQ block across articles.
4. Do not add `FAQPage` JSON-LD. The visible answers exist for readers and possible passage retrieval, not a promised rich result.
5. Every article links upward to its hub through breadcrumbs and laterally only when the relationship is explained.
6. Avoid sexualised title tags, descriptions and imagery on `/practice/unbound-chest/`. The page is about autonomy and religious authority.
7. Do not target the names of living or real religions as comparison keywords.
8. When commissioned images exist, use descriptive filenames and supplied alt text without stuffing the target query.

## Measurement

Track:

- indexed URLs and chosen canonicals;
- branded impressions for Rose Mothers, Ash Mothers, Ash Order and Unbound Chest;
- clicks from `/faith/` into practice and institution clusters;
- scroll and related-link engagement on long articles;
- assisted visits to `/books/the-ashen-flame/`;
- external links to the exact rank table, visitor guide and institutional explanations.

The early objective is entity ownership and a coherent knowledge graph. Broader acquisition pages can later translate these concepts into non-brand editorial queries without weakening the canonical lore pages.
