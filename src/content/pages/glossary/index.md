> **Fiction notice:** These terms belong to the invented Ateshi world of *The Ashen Flame*. Definitions avoid major plot spoilers.

This glossary provides the canonical public meaning of recurring Ateshi terms. A word may link to a longer page where doctrine, law or historical practice requires more nuance.

## A

### Anousheh

A senior Rose Mother and one of the most revered religious figures of her generation. Her institutional relationships become the subject of Farah Kianzad's investigation. [Read the spoiler-safe dossier.](/people/anousheh/)

### Ateshi

A follower of Ateshism, or the adjective for a culture, belief, institution or practice associated with the religion. Use **Ateshi society**, not “Atesh society.”

### Ateshism

The fictional monotheistic religion at the centre of *The Ashen Flame*. Its formal name is *Din-e Atezhan*, the Faith of the Flame. [What Is Ateshism?](/faith/what-is-ateshism/)

### Ash House

A residence, training centre, archive or office associated with the Ash Order. Ash spaces tend towards controlled access and practical austerity rather than the pastoral character of Rose institutions.

### Ash Mother

A fully qualified or ordained investigator within the Ash Order. The title names a professional vocation, not necessarily a senior commander. [Meet the Ash Mothers.](/institutions/ash-mothers/)

### Ash Order

The separate Ateshi institution responsible for investigating religious authority, Temple accounts and systemic failures. It does not function as ordinary police. [Explore the Ash Order.](/institutions/ash-order/)

## B

### Bactria

A Central Asian Compact republic beyond the upper Oxus. It includes Balkh and much of the northern plain that belongs to Afghanistan in real-world geography.

### Book of Fire

The first Ateshi sacred book, formally the *Book of Fire—The Sayings of Arahita*. It was compiled during Mehr Arahita's lifetime. [Read about the Book of Fire.](/faith/book-of-fire/)

### Book of the Hidden Flame

The second Ateshi sacred book, made public roughly three centuries after Arahita. Whether it preserves her concealed teaching or a later institutional composition remains disputed. [Explore the controversy.](/faith/book-of-hidden-flame/)

## C

### Common Ember

A Temple contribution system recorded through household declarations. It supports services and institutional wealth but is not collected as a criminal tax. Social pressure may still make nominally voluntary participation complicated.

### Common Persian

The shared language of Compact government, education, trade, scripture, interregional worship and Ash records. Many citizens speak another language at home.

### Compact, the

The transnational structure connecting independent Ateshi-majority states through shared arrangements for movement, trade and professional recognition. [Explore the Compact.](/world/compact/)

### Consent as Cosmic Principle

The fifth Ateshi pillar: the claim that a genuine refusal outranks blessing, status, desire or sacred purpose. It is associated most fully with the *Book of the Hidden Flame*. [Read about consent.](/faith/consent/)

### Council Ash

One of the small number of senior Ash officers serving in central Order governance, immediately below the Keeper of Ashes in the formal hierarchy.

## D

### Dargaz

A mountain town in northern Khorasan and Farah Kianzad's first Ash posting.

### Divine Immanence

The first Ateshi pillar: the Divine is present within existence rather than separated from it. [Explore the Eternal Flame.](/faith/eternal-flame/)

## E

### Eternal Flame

The central Ateshi sign of divine presence, illumination and transformation. A physical sacred flame is not itself God. [Read about the Eternal Flame.](/faith/eternal-flame/)

## F

### Farah Kianzad

The Ash Mother and central character of *The Ashen Flame*. She is twenty-eight when the Anousheh referral reaches her in spring 2010. [Read Farah's dossier.](/people/farah-kianzad/)

### First Burning

Mehr Arahita's founding experience of recognising divine presence beside a household fire after the death of her infant daughter. It is presented as the first documented recognition, not a unique divine arrival.

### First Investigator

A senior Ash rank responsible for leading major or complex investigative work. It stands above Senior Ash and below Provincial Ash.

### First Witness

The Ateshi title for Mehr Arahita. She is neither a goddess nor an incarnation; she is the first recorded person to articulate the experience later called the First Burning.

### Five Pillars

Divine Immanence, Sacred Embodiment, Presence as Purification, the Hierarchy of Love and Consent as Cosmic Principle. [Explore all five.](/faith/five-pillars/)

## H

### Hierarchy of Love

The Ateshi pillar that tests love through the care, responsibility and material action it produces rather than through sentiment alone.

## K

### Keeper of Ashes

The head of the Ash Order and highest office in its nine-stage command structure.

### Khorasan

Persia's northeastern highland province, extending towards the Kopet Dag and Central Asian routes. It is not a separate country.

### Khwarazm

A Central Asian Compact republic along the lower Oxus.

## M

### Mehr Arahita

The ninth-century First Witness associated with the founding of Ateshism and the sayings in the *Book of Fire*. [Read her biography.](/faith/mehr-arahita/)

## N

### Night of Ashes

An observance on the longest night. Households lower their lamps, carry names of the dead to be burned unread in communal flame, take fresh light home and eat together until midnight.

## P

### Persia

Farah's country and a major Ateshi centre. It is a constitutional monarchy with Tehran as capital and Khorasan as a northeastern province. [Explore Persia.](/world/persia/)

### Prayer urn

A vessel used for burning private written griefs, wishes, shames or names as an act of honest naming and release. [Read about the prayer urn.](/practice/prayer-urn/)

### Presence as Purification

The Ateshi pillar holding that sustained attention can expose self-deception and make moral action possible. Attention does not grant permission to intrude.

### Provincial Ash

An Ash officer responsible for the Order's work across a province, positioned above First Investigator and below Council Ash.

## R

### Rose Mother

A member of Ateshism's historically female priesthood. Rose Mothers work across worship, pastoral care, medicine, education, theology, finance and administration. Rose skin is unmarked. [Meet the Rose Mothers.](/institutions/rose-mothers/)

## S

### Sacred Circle

An Ateshi marriage that assumes no fixed ending. It can be dissolved; permanence is an intended promise rather than an irreversible legal fact.

### Sacred Embodiment

The second Ateshi pillar, teaching that the body can disclose truths about divine presence, creation, care and change. Traditional claims about uniquely female manifestation remain contested. [Explore the theology of the body.](/faith/body/)

### Sister-Bond

A sacred and civilly recognised relationship between women, sometimes existing within a plural household. [Read about Sister-Bonds.](/society/sister-bonds/)

### Sogdiana

A Central Asian Compact republic centred around Samarkand and Bukhara. A Sogdian district referral begins the Anousheh investigation.

## T

### Temple system

The Ateshi network of worship spaces and service institutions, including schools, clinics, kitchens, archives and welfare. [Explore the Temple system.](/institutions/temple-system/)

### Temporary Marriage

A recognised Ateshi marriage with a term and expiry date declared in advance. Parenthood and other obligations may survive the relationship. [Read about Ateshi marriage.](/society/marriage/)

### Tehran

The capital of Persia and Farah Kianzad's childhood home.

### Tus

The provincial capital of Khorasan and the rail connection from which Farah continues by road to Dargaz.

## U

### Unbound Chest

An Ateshi doctrine protecting a woman's choice concerning support, coverage and exposure. “Unbound” means uncoerced, not unsupported. [Read the full explanation.](/practice/unbound-chest/)

## W

### Wednesday

The principal Ateshi communal holy day, often marked by shared cleaning, worship, teaching, movement and a communal meal. [Explore Wednesday worship.](/practice/wednesday/)

## Z

### Zarrindokht

The early organiser remembered as Ateshism's Second Founder. She formalised training, accounts, provincial houses, ordination and service records after the first community.

## A note on terminology

Older drafts and exploratory conversations used several names that are not current canon. The website follows the publication master: **Ateshism** is the religion, **Ateshi** is its adjective and follower, **Persia** is Farah's country, and the **Compact** is the transnational political structure.

If a term appears in the novel but not here, the absence may protect a spoiler or indicate that its public definition still requires canon review.
