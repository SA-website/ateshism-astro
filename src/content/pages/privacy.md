## What this page covers

Ateshism.com is a fan-facing worldbuilding site for the novel *The Ashen Flame* by Alexei Sassani. This page explains the cookies and analytics used on the site.

## Cookies we use

We use a small number of cookies, only after you accept the cookie banner:

- **Google Analytics (GA4)** — helps us understand which pages readers visit and how they navigate the site, so we can improve it. Analytics data is anonymised at the IP level.
- **Google Search Console** — does not set a cookie on your device. It only shows us aggregate data about how the site appears in Google Search results.

We do not use cookies for advertising, and we do not sell or share visitor data with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Your choices

When you first visit the site, you can **Accept** or **Decline** analytics cookies via the banner. If you decline, no analytics cookie is set. You can clear your browser's local storage for this site at any time to reset that choice and see the banner again.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the site's contact address, listed on the author's main site.
