The books of the Ateshi world explore a civilisation shaped by the belief that the Divine is present in bodies, relationships and public life—and by institutions that do not always survive their own ideals.

The series begins with *The Ashen Flame*, a novel of investigation, family memory and religious power by Alexei Sassani. Future titles and short fiction will share the same world without requiring readers to study an encyclopaedia before opening the story.

> **Fiction notice:** Ateshism and the Ateshi world are invented. The books use real geographical names within an alternate history, but they do not describe a real religion or historical civilisation.

## The Ashen Flame

**Khorasan, spring 2010.** Ash Mother Farah Kianzad receives a referral concerning Anousheh, one of Ateshism's most revered modern Rose Mothers.

The allegation is not that Anousheh loved too many people. Ateshi law recognises temporary marriage, and eleven relationships are not themselves a crime or scandal. The problem lies in the overlap: pastoral counsel, housing, appointments and professional influence appear in some of the same files.

Farah must determine where care ended, authority began and a civilisation's admiration made the difference impossible to record.

[*The Ashen Flame* book page](/books/the-ashen-flame/) includes the full spoiler-safe description, themes and verified publication information. [Read an extract](/books/the-ashen-flame/extract/) from Chapter One.

## What kind of novel is it?

*The Ashen Flame* combines:

- literary fantasy grounded in character and social consequence;
- alternate history using Persia, Khorasan and Central Asian geography;
- institutional investigation based on records, testimony and conflicting interpretations;
- family drama shaped by love, consent and unequal status;
- theology treated as a living intellectual system rather than decorative lore.

The novel's central mystery is moral and evidential. It does not ask only what happened. It asks whether an institution can identify coercion when nobody behaved like a simple villain, and whether genuine good can coexist with unexamined power.

## Do I need to read the website first?

No. The novel introduces its own world through Farah's life and work. Ateshism.com is a companion archive for readers who want to go deeper.

Before reading, the [Start Here guide](/start/) provides a short, spoiler-safe orientation. During or after reading, the glossary and linked articles explain terms without turning the story into homework.

## Spoiler policy

Book and character pages use four levels:

- **S0:** safe world information;
- **S1:** premise and early-book context;
- **S2:** discoveries made during the investigation;
- **S3:** the ending and later consequences.

Search descriptions, cards and structured data stay at S0 or S1. S2 and S3 material will not be placed into indexable HTML merely because a visitor can press a reveal button.

## Future books

The site has been designed for a continuing Ateshi series, including later novels and shorter works of history, romance and everyday life. No unannounced title, date or plot description is being treated as final canon.

When another book is confirmed, it will receive its own permanent page while people, places and institutions remain part of the shared world archive. This avoids rebuilding the site around each new novel and allows a character or location page to list every verified appearance.
